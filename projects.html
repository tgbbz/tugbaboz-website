Research and product development projects

CyberAI Innovation: Expanding Workforce Pathways into Cybersecurity and AI

Funded by the National Science Foundation (Award No. 2622977, 2026–2029), this project brings AI and cybersecurity into career and technical education (CTE) in North Dakota. AI is entering agriculture, health care, and other sectors that rural economies depend on, and more of the work in these sectors involves overseeing automated systems. CTE teachers take part in summer professional learning and monthly lesson design sessions, and then they teach the units they design. The project is also developing an AI and cybersecurity credential for high school students. I lead the project as principal investigator with colleagues in education and engineering at UND.

AI-Powered Teacher Support System for Classroom Talk

This proof-of-concept project develops an AI-powered system that analyzes teacher-student talk in rural elementary classrooms. Teachers receive a report on patterns in their questioning and feedback, which they can use to reflect on and adjust their instruction. The project is funded by UND’s Early Career Scholars Program, and I lead it as principal investigator.

STEM Strong

Funded by the National Science Foundation (DRK–12), STEM STRONG provides supports to teachers and promotes science and engineering in elementary classrooms.

My Home, My Health

Supported by the NIH SEPA program, this initiative empowers students to explore disease ecology concepts in their communities.
