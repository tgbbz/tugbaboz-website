Projects

CyberAI Innovation: Expanding Workforce Pathways into Cybersecurity and AI

Funded by the National Science Foundation (Award No. 2622977, 2026–2029), this project brings AI and cybersecurity into career and technical education (CTE) in North Dakota. CTE teachers take part in summer professional learning and monthly lesson design sessions and then teach the units they design. The project is also developing an AI and cybersecurity credential for high school students. I lead the project as principal investigator with colleagues in education and engineering at UND.

AI-Powered Teacher Support System for Classroom Talk

Funded by UND’s Early Career Scholars Program, this proof-of-concept study develops an AI-powered system that analyzes teacher-student talk in rural elementary classrooms. Teachers receive a report on patterns in their questioning and feedback that they can use to reflect on their instruction. I lead the study as principal investigator.

STEM Strong

Funded by the National Science Foundation (DRK–12), STEM STRONG provides supports to teachers and promotes science and engineering in elementary classrooms.

My Home, My Health

Supported by the NIH SEPA program, this initiative empowers students to explore disease ecology concepts in their communities.
